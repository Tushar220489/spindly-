Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense records you create — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and session activity, needed to keep the Service running securely.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to provide core features of the Service: authenticating your account, storing and displaying your expenses, and generating summaries and category breakdowns. We do not use your financial data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and are accessible only through your authenticated session. We retain your data for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time through your account settings.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on limited third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting or email delivery) solely to operate the Service, and any such providers are only given the access necessary to perform those functions.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to the Spendly team through your account settings.
